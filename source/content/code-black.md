## Anaesthetic Considerations

**Code Black** is declared pre- or in- hospital if:

- History of **head trauma**
- **GCS ≤ 8**
- Signs of **raised ICP**
    - Fixed and dilated pupil(s)
    - Cushing’s Reflex: hypertension and bradycardia

### 2222 “Code Black trauma team to A&E Resus”

> Attendance as for advanced trauma team plus:
>
> - **Neurosurgery**
> - **1220 Anaesthetist**

- If **haemorrhagic shock** and a transient or non-responder to volume replacement, then **haemorrhage control must take priority.**
- In lesser cases, decide on which is the most urgent pathology.
- **Avoid concurrent Code Red and Code Black.** Mortality in this group is devastating. TTL/Trauma Surgeon/Neurosurgeon/Anaesthetist MDT consultant discussion if unclear.

## ICP Care

- Adequate sedation, analgesia and paralysis
- 15-30<sup>O</sup> head up (tilt the whole bed/trolley if spine not cleared)
- Head to midline neutral position
- Tape ETT: avoid ties around the neck
- Remove cervical collar (use blocks/tape for intubated patients until c-spine cleared)

### Targets

| Target | |
|---|---|
| **MAP** | **> 90 mmHg** (CPP > 60 if ICP known)<br>Insert an arterial line when able, but do not delay interventions |
| **PaO<sub>2</sub>** | **> 13 kPa** (spO2 >95%) |
| **PaCO<sub>2</sub>** | **4.5-5 kPa**, titrate to EtCO2<br>Initially assume difference of 1kPa between EtCO2 & PaCO2, confirm with blood gasses |
| **PEEP** | **5 cmH<sub>2</sub>O**, Vt: 4-8 ml/kg |
| **Blood glucose** | **6-10 mmol/L** |
| **Temperature** | **36.5-37.5°C** |
| **Sodium** | **135-155 mmol/L** |

- **If critical ICP** (blown pupil or ICP > 25 mmHg if measured)
    - 1st line osmotherapy: Hypertonic saline (6 ml/kg 5% to a maximum of 350mls)
    - 2nd line: mannitol 2.5 ml/kg 20% or 5 ml/kg 10%
    - Immediate neurosurgical review
- **Treat seizures**:
    - Levetiracetam (Keppra) 60 mg/kg up to 4500 mg max. To be administered in a 100 ml bag of 0.9% Saline over ~ 10 min.

## Assessment and Actions in ED

- Full trauma primary survey and resuscitation
- Intubate (if not already intubated)
    - Usual drugs and doses: **Fentanyl** 1-3 mcg/kg, **Ketamine** 2 mg/kg, **Rocuronium** 1 mg/kg
    - Continue sedation, analgesia and blood pressure support
    - Maintain Manual In-Line Stabilisation if C-Spine not cleared
- Initiate ICP care
- Blood gas and ROTEM
    - Do not delay for arterial line.
    - Trauma induced coagulopathy is common.
- Ensure 2 grams of **Tranexamic Acid** given if within 2 hours of injury
- CT pan-scan to identify all injuries:
    - Include: head, neck, chest, abdomen and pelvis.
    - Neurosurgery/Radiologist may consider CT angio/venogram
- Neurosurgery:
    - Immediate decision on theatre
    - Clear C-Spine to assist ICP care
- Radiology:
    - Primary survey initial CT report to identify other critical injury
- Immediate transfer to theatre when plan confirmed
- Consider ordering Pack A for all Code Black cases

### Anticoagulant & Antiplatelet Drugs

- Do not wait for lab results before reversing anticoagulation
- **Haematology SpR bleep 1155** (in hours, via switch out-of-hours)
- **Haematology lab: ext 61049, 60342** out of hours
- Follow trust guidelines for anticoagulant reversal (**WeShare** → search → ‘‘over-anticoagulation’’)
- If other coagulopathy (e.g. haemophilia, von Willebrands) discuss with haematology.

## In theatre

- Continue ICP care
- Use **Code Black** timeout/ checklist
- Check:
    - ETT is secure
    - Temperature probe
    - Eyes are protected/padded/sealed
    - Head up 15-30<sup>o</sup>
    - Urinary catheter
    - Flowtrons +/- TED stockings
    - Warming
    - Pressure areas
- Maintain anaesthesia: volatile or propofol, plus opioid.
    - Remifentanil (Ct > 3ng/ml or ~ 0.1 mcg/kg/min infusion) or
    - Fentanyl (e.g. 3 mcg/kg bolus then 4 mcg/kg/h infusion)
- **Metaraminol** or **Noradrenaline** to keep **MAP > 90 mmHg**
- Arterial line when able
- Large bore IV access
- Consider Belmont for all cases
- Antibiotics: Cefuroxime 1.5g IV unless allergy
- **1-2 litres blood loss is common**. Beware escalating vasopressors and actively look for blood loss.
- ABG and ROTEM guided resuscitation
- Repeat TXA and antibiotics if blood loss > 3 litres

> If skull not open at **30 min from knife-to-skin**, discuss with surgeon.

## Post operative considerations

- Confirm full CT report – look for missed injuries.
- ACCU level 3 care:
    - Maintain sedation and analgesia
- Change ETT if flexi/reinforced:
    - Use ACCU subglottic suction port tube if possible.
- If time in theatre:
    - CVC (femoral or subclavian preferred, jugular acceptable).
    - Naso- or oro-gastric **feeding tube** (from ACCU), **beware base of skull fracture.**
