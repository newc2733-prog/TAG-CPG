## Resus (Bay 8)

Bay 8 in Resus is the default bay for patients with major traumatic injury including neurological injury. In addition to the standard setup of a Resus bay it includes:

- REBOA trolley
- Difficult airway trolley
- ParaPAC ventilator, Mapleson C circuit attached to wall O<sub>2</sub>, suction
- Inditherm warming mattress
- CODE RED gratnel trolley
- Belmont Rapid Infusion device
- Surgical lighting overhead
- X-ray gantry

## Difficult Airway Trolley

The Difficult Airway Trolley is located between Bay 7 and 8 at the patient’s head end of the bay. It follows the Difficult Airway Society Plan A / B / C / D approach and contains:

- **Plan A** - Various sizes of ETT including those with a subglottic suction port, catheter mount / filter sets, direct laryngoscopy with Macintosh 3 and 4 blades, tube-ties
- **Plan B** - iGel size 3 / 4 / 5, LMA supreme size 3 / 4 / 5
- **Plan C** - Self-inflating bag, Guedel airways size 2 / 3 / 4, face masks size 3 / 4 / 5. Sugammadex emergency reversal (16mg/kg) is very rarely an option for trauma patients as their ongoing care usually requires intubation. **Failure here will immediately lead to Plan D.**
- **Plan D** - Front of neck access equipment (scalpel / bougie / tube), **(see [Emergency Department RSI: Plan D](#ed-rsi.s5))**, various sizes of tracheostomy
