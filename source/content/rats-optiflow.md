## Definition

Optiflow / high flow oxygenation therapy (HFOT) / high flow nasal oxygenation (HFNO) allows the administration of high F<sub>i</sub>O<sub>2</sub> (up to 1.0), high flow, humidified, heated oxygen therapy to a patient via wide bore nasal cannulae. The high flow (70L/min<sup>-1</sup>) administered aims to match the high peak inspiratory flow rates of the patient in respiratory failure to maximise F<sub>i</sub>O<sub>2</sub>. It may also deliver a variable amount of PEEP.

## Indications

- Hypoxic respiratory failure (CAP, asthma, PE, etc)
- Pre-oxygenation prior to intubation
- Apnoeic oxygenation
- Post-extubation hypoxia

## Tips and tricks

- Theatre Optiflow will deliver a fixed F<sub>i</sub>O<sub>2</sub> 1.0 with a titratable flow
- Consider intubation in theatre if anticipated difficult airway
- Filled with sterile water, **not** 0.9% NaCl

## Contraindications

- Epistaxis
- Base of skull fracture
- Recent nasal or upper aero-digestive tract surgery
- Nasal obstruction

## Complications

- Local trauma / discomfort / pressure areas
- Epistaxis
- Gastric distension
