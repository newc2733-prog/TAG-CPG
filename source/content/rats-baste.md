## BASTE

BASTE is a helpful mnemonic that acts as a reminder of the near constant reassessment patients require during a CODE RED. It includes assessment of ongoing transfusion requirements, the metabolic status of the patient including reversible causes of coagulopathy (acidosis, hypocalcaemia, hypothermia) as well as encouraging frequent communication with the surgical team. **Review BASTE out loud to the whole operating theatre.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **B**lood | Review the transfusion board, what has been given? What is the anticipated ongoing requirement? ROTEM results? Do you need to order more? |
| **A**cid / base balance | pH, lactate, HCO<sub>3</sub><sup>-</sup> / BE |
| **S**urgical progress | Communicate with the surgical team, do you need time to catch up with blood products (‘pack and hold’) or otherwise optimise the patient for the next stage of surgery? |
| **T**emperature<br>**T**ime | Patient temperature & trend, time elapsed since start of case. Ambient temperature at maximum (25°C), active warming of patient with Bair hugger / Inditherm mattress. |
| **E**lectrolytes | K<sup>+</sup><5.8mmol/L, corrected Ca<sup>2+</sup>>1.0mmol/L, correct and replace as required. |
