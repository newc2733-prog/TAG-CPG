## Definition

The Belmont infusion device is able to infuse a maximum of 750mL/min of fluid or blood products whilst protecting against air embolus, warming the fluid to 37.5°C and managing line pressure to prevent vessel trauma.

## Clinical use

The Belmont is set up in ED for CODE RED. This will accompany the patient to theatre and continue to be used. Another unit is sent back to Resus with the ED monitor when the patient is handed over in theatre.

## Tips and tricks

- Manufacturer advises all blood products can be given **except** Platelets and all fluids except those containing calcium.
- When unplugged it reverts to battery mode which has a maximum infusion rate of 50ml/min with no heating function. Bear this in mind when in transit.
- The machine will generally prompt you how to correct errors - first step is to look at what the screen is telling you to do.
- Prime with 0.9% NaCl only.
- There is no heating function at flow rate <5mL/min.
- Bolus functions allows delivery of 500mL aliquots, a useful function, and volumes can be altered if required.

## Belmont bucket

- 3L capacity and blood products can be emptied directly into bucket.
- Not usually setup in ED, consider adding to Belmont in theatre.
- Most useful if anticipating sudden or torrential blood loss.
- Cell salvage can be plugged directly into the top of the bucket.
- Beware kinking of tubing below bucket.
