## Transport Ventilators

Within Resus, ACCU and Theatres the most common transport ventilator used is the Oxylog 3000+. It is able to provide both volume and pressure controlled mandatory modes of ventilation as well as modes for spontaneously breathing patients (CPAP and SIMV / PC-BiPAP). In the Trauma Bay of Resus (Bay 8) the paraPAC is the ventilator used.

## Tips and tricks

- Ventilation can have significant impact on haemodynamics - consider this carefully at initial setup
- Low PEEP + low tidal volume with permissive hypercapnia may reduce impact on cardiovascular stability
- Balancing neuroprotective ventilation with the haemodynamics of a shocked patient is challenging - this is a senior-led discussion
- The Oxylog 3000+ has a tidal volume (Vt) range of 50 - 2000mL, paediatric circuits should be used for Vt < 250mL
