One method of reducing vessel injury risk is to not completely push the dilator through the line (*Picture 2*).

The larger bore (brown) port should be used for product resuscitation and the smaller (white) for drug administration. We advise a three way tap to be connected as below (*Picture 3*).

## Arterial lines

An arterial line is a useful addition to the monitoring of a trauma patient and attempts to insert one should be made early on during treatment. It should not delay potentially life saving interventions as non-invasive BP (NIBP), or even a peripheral / central pulse palpation can provide similar information, far more quickly. A venous gas may also be obtained from the trauma line to guide biochemical resuscitation. Securing an arterial line can prove challenging in the hypotensive trauma patient. We recommend securing the transducer for the arterial line to the NIBP cuff (see picture), as this will securely keep it at the correct level.
