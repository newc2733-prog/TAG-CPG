### Introduction

The Resuscitation, Anaesthetic and Trauma Skills (RATS) handbook is the accompanying text to the RATS Course. It is designed as an aide-memoire to allow rapid access to key practical knowledge for those involved in the acute management of traumatically injured patients.

This handbook will support less experienced members of a trauma team to participate as active members and make effective contributions to trauma patient care.

It does not replace the knowledge gained through experience or shared by fellow team members but is intended to augment learning on the journey to trauma care mastery.
