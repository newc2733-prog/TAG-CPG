## Activation

Initial awareness of a major incident may be via Social Media or news outlets.

Begin preparations to attend but await instructions. This is likely to be via text message in the first instance via Page One to inform Consultant Anaesthetists and Surgeons. **HOSPITAL ID IS ESSENTIAL FOR ENTRY.**

Upon arrival get into scrubs and present yourself to the Tea Bar.

## Process

Theatre 12 continues with ‘life or limb’ surgery and all other theatres will finish their current operations as soon as possible and begin to prepare to receive casualties.

At a minimum, theatres 7 - 12 will be made ready as for a CODE RED and Theatre 11 as for a CODE BLACK.

Additional space will be made ready in Theatres 1 – 6 and then ACAD as necessary for additional patients who should ideally be more stable than those managed in 7 - 12.

Theatre staff will cohort and collect in the Tea Bar.

## Where

Oncall trauma, vascular, general, orthopaedic and OMFS surgeons will report directly to Resus with the on-call ITU, Anaesthetic and ODP teams.

All other staff will report to Tea Bar and await instructions.

## Communication

The Anaesthetic Coordinator and Theatre Coordinator will be the central point of all communication and allocation of roles.

Try to minimise any unnecessary communication and beware of failure of mobile phones.
