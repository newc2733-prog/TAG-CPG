## Core Skills

- Standardised resuscitation of Code Red/ Traumatic Cardiac Arrest
- Standardised resuscitation of Code Black
- Standardised resuscitation of the Paediatric/ Pregnant/ Elderly Trauma patient
- Airway management of trauma patients including:
    - Emergency Department RSI & Front-of-neck access
    - Difficult Airway & OMFS Trauma
    - Penetrating neck injury
    - Placement of double lumen tube & one-lung ventilation
- Advanced vascular access skills (including placement of subclavian trauma line)
- Interpretation and use of ROTEM and familiarity with emergency reversal of anticoagulation medications
- Standardised approach to analgesia for acute trauma pain management
- Standardised approach and ability to deliver the following regional anaesthesia:
    - Supra- or Infra-clavicular block
    - Femoral & fascia iliaca block
    - Sciatic nerve block
    - Epidural, paravertebral, serratus anterior, erector spinae blocks
    - Placement of regional anaesthetic nerve catheters
- Understanding of principles involved in resuscitation of Burns, Drowning & Hypothermia, Spinal Cord Injury
- Understanding of principles involved in resuscitation involving Thoracotomy, REBOA, ECMO, Peri-mortem Caesarean Section
- Understanding of principles involved in Major Incident Management
- Familiarity with TAG Clinical Practice Guidelines and Resuscitation, Anaesthesia and Trauma Skills (RATS) Workbook
- High-stakes decision making and non-technical skills appropriate to leadership and followership

## Specialist Clinical Skills

*(to exist within the group but not all individuals)*

- Interpretation of eFAST Scan in Trauma and relevant Point-of-Care Ultrasound
- Conceptual understanding of ECMO/ eCPR and ability to support ECMO Team
- Conceptual understanding of REBOA and ability to support REBOA Team
- Interpretation of Trauma CT scan
- Placement of intercostal drains
- RATS Course Faculty

## Aspirational/ Future-intended Skills

- Member of delivery team for ECMO/ eCPR
- Higher complexity Regional Anaesthesia
- Trauma Team Leader Course Faculty
