## Inditherm mattress tips and tricks

- The Inditherm mattress is used wire side up, is X-ray compatible and can also be used as a transfer sheet. Radiology will not currently CT patients on the mattress.
- Setting up and initiate before patient arrival.
- It will function fully for 30min in battery mode.
- Look for green light to confirm it is turned on.
- Any of the ports on the front of the control unit can be used.
- The maximum temperature is 40°C and will not cause burns but will take **up to 20mins** to reach operating temperature.
- A Bair hugger placed on top of the Inditherm will speed the warming process.
