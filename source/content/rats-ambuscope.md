## Definition

The ambuscope is a single-use, disposable fiberoptic bronchoscope available in 3 sizes - ‘slim’, ‘regular’ and ‘large’. It can be used to guide the insertion of an ETT and perform bronchoalveolar lavage. The larger sizes (Regular [Green] and Large [Orange]) provide the strongest suction.

![](fig/rats-ambuscope-2.jpg)

## Uses in Trauma

- “Double setup” for neck injuries with suspicion of airway injury (combined with RSI and videolaryngoscopy)
- Double lumen tube confirmation and monitoring
- iGel airway exchange

## Size

| Size | Slim | Regular | Large |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Colour** | Grey | Green | Orange |
| **Suction Channel (mm)** | 1.2 | 2.2 | 2.8 |
| **Minimum diameter ETT (ID)** | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| **Suggested ETT (ID)** | 6 | 7 | 8 |
